# People balance joint reward, fairness and complexity to develop social norms in a two-player game

*Yu, D., &amp; Thompson, B. D. (2024).* Proc. 46th CogSci.

[PsyArXiv](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/qe6bp)

## Why it's here

Pairs converge on norms that trade off three things at once — how much the pair earns, how evenly it is split, and how simple the rule is to state. The complexity term is the interesting one: it suggests joint plans are selected partly for describability, not only for payoff.
