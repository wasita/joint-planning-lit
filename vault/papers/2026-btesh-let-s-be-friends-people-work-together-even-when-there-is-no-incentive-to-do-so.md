# Let's be friends! People work together even when there is no incentive to do so

*Btesh, V., &amp; Ho, M. K. (2026).* Proc. 48th CogSci (in press).

[author page](https://vbtesh.github.io/)

## Why it's here

People choose to work together in the absence of any material reason to. If that holds up, it is a direct challenge to accounts on which joint action is explained by payoff structure — team reasoning, virtual bargaining and joint-intention models all predict cooperation <em>because</em> it pays, and would need something further to explain cooperating when it does not. Listed from the first author's own page; the proceedings were not yet posted at the time of writing.
