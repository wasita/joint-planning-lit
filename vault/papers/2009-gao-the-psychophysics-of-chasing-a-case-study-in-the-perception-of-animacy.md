# The psychophysics of chasing: A case study in the perception of animacy

*Gao, T., Newman, G. E., &amp; Scholl, B. J. (2009).* Cognitive Psychology, 59(2), 154–179.

[PDF](https://taogao.comm.ucla.edu/static/pdf/The%20psychophysics%20of%20chasing_Gao%20Newman%20Scholl_2009.pdf)

## Why it's here

Chasing is detected rapidly and automatically from motion alone. In continuous spatial tasks this matters more than it first appears: partners are reading intentions off trajectories perceptually, below the level of anything that deserves to be called inference — which is a competing explanation for behaviour a planning model would otherwise claim.
