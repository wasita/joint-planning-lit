# Observing joint action: Coordination creates commitment

*Michael, J., Sebanz, N., &amp; Knoblich, G. (2016).* Cognition, 157, 106–113.

[PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5146639/)

## Why it's here

The empirical half, and the direct precursor to third-party commitment-inference work: observers attribute commitment on the basis of observed coordination alone, with no agreement having been made. Establishes the effect that later computational accounts try to explain.
