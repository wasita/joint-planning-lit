# Learning to cooperate with humans using generative agents

*Liang, Y., Chen, D., Gupta, A., Du, S. S., &amp; Jaques, N. (2024).* NeurIPS 2024.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13934)

## Why it's here

The direct successor to the Overcooked human-AI coordination result: train a generative latent-variable model of human partner strategies, then train a cooperator against samples from it. Evaluated with real human teammates rather than proxies, which is rarer in this literature than it should be.
