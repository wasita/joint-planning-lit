# Interaction structure constrains the emergence of conventions in group communication

*Boyce, V., Hawkins, R. D., Goodman, N. D., &amp; Frank, M. C. (2024).* PNAS, 121, e2403888121.

[PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11252989/)

## Why it's here

Large synchronous online reference games in groups of two to six, showing that <em>who talks to whom, in what order</em> determines whether a shared convention emerges at all. Both a result about coordination and a working demonstration of how to run multiplayer experiments at scale.
