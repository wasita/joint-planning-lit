# Game theory of mind

*Yoshida, Dolan & Friston (2008).* PLOS Computational Biology, 4(12), e1000254.

[PLOS (open access)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1000254)

## Why it's here

The recursive-ToM counterpart to Camerer et al.'s cognitive hierarchy, arrived at independently in neuroscience: a k-th order player models a (k−1)-order opponent's value function, with the regress bottomed out by bounded rationality rather than an infinite tower of beliefs. Read alongside Camerer–Ho–Chong — same move (truncate the recursion), same year the field converged on it (economics: 2004; here: 2008), different bottoming-out mechanism (Poisson-weighted step distribution vs. a hard sophistication ceiling K). Devaine et al. (2014, thread C) builds directly on this k-ToM formalism.
