# The psychology of coordination and common knowledge

*Thomas, DeScioli, Haque & Pinker (2014).* JPSP, 107(4), 657–676.

[PDF](https://pdescioli.com/papers/thomas.etal.common.knowledge.jpsp14.pdf)

## Why it's here

One paper, one finding, and it constrains the model class before a line of it is written: people treat common knowledge as a categorical state, not a point on a probability continuum. That rules out an entire family of graded-higher-order-belief models that would otherwise be tempting to write down. Cheap insurance.
