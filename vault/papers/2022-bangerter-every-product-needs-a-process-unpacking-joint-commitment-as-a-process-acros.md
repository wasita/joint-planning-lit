# Every product needs a process: Unpacking joint commitment as a process across species

*Bangerter, A., Genty, E., Heesen, R., Rossano, F., &amp; Zuberbühler, K. (2022).* Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B, 377(1859), 20210095.

[open access](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2021.0095)

## Why it's here

Argues that joint commitment should be modelled as a <em>process</em> — entry, maintenance, exit — rather than a state that either holds or does not. That reframing matters for continuous-time paradigms, where commitment is precisely something that builds and decays over a trajectory.
