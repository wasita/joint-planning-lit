# Reward of labor coordination and hunting success in wild chimpanzees

*Samuni, L., Preis, A., Deschner, T., Crockford, C., &amp; Wittig, R. M. (2018).* Communications Biology, 1(1), 138.

[open access](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-018-0142-3)

## Why it's here

Hunt participation predicts meat received, independent of begging — evidence that payoff structure sustains cooperative hunting in the wild, and a natural counterpart to the payoff manipulations used in laboratory stag hunts.
