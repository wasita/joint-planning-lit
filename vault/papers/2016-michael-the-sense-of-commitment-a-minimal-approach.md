# The sense of commitment: A minimal approach

*Michael, J., Sebanz, N., &amp; Knoblich, G. (2016).* Frontiers in Psychology, 6, 1968.

[open access](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.01968)

## Why it's here

The framework half of a pair. Argues for a <em>sense</em> of commitment assembled from minimal cues — repetition, effort, coordination itself — rather than from explicit promising or full mentalizing. This is the account a computational model of commitment inference is implicitly formalizing.
