## Why it's here

<code>memo</code> is a probabilistic programming language purpose-built for reasoning about reasoning — nested theory of mind written declaratively and compiled to run fast. Infrastructure rather than theory, and the most practical thing on this list for anyone who actually has to implement recursive partner models.
