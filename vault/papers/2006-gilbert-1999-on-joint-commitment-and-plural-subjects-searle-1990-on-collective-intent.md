# 

*Gilbert (1999) on joint commitment and plural subjects · Searle (1990) on collective intentions · Bacharach (2006).*

## Why it's here

The philosophical backdrop Kuang et al. builds on. Worth knowing the positions; reading them in full is not urgent. Gilbert in particular is the source of “joint commitment” as a technical term, and her account is notably stronger than Bratman's — obligations that can't be unilaterally rescinded — which matters for modeling the normative reactions (anger, guilt) that Kuang et al. measure.
