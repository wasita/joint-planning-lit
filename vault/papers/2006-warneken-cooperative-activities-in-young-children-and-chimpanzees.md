# Cooperative activities in young children and chimpanzees

*Warneken, F., Chen, F., &amp; Tomasello, M. (2006).* Child Development, 77(3), 640–663.

## Why it's here

The phylogenetic companion to Gräfenhain et al.: when a partner stops participating, children re-engage them communicatively, while chimpanzees do not and treat only the instrumental tasks as worth resuming. Read for the comparative contrast rather than for a measure of commitment as such.
