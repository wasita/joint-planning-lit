# People construct simplified mental representations to plan

*Ho, Abel, Correa, Littman, Cohen & Griffiths (2022).* Nature, 606, 129–136.

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06948)

## Why it's here

The flagship. Figures-first is fine — the construct (value-guided construal: people build a simplified task representation before planning over it) matters more here than the methods. The open question worth turning over: what's the joint analogue of a construal? Do two people converge on a shared simplification, and does coordination succeed or fail on that convergence?
