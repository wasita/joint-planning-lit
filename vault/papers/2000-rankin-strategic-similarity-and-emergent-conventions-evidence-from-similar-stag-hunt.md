# Strategic similarity and emergent conventions: evidence from similar stag hunt games

*Rankin, Van Huyck & Battalio (2000).* GEB, 32(2), 315–337.

## Why it's here

Conventions emerge in half of cohorts even with payoffs and labels scrambled every round — and when they do, they track efficiency rather than security or risk dominance. Evidence that people abstract strategic structure across surface variation, i.e. the “abstract goals” claim arriving from the economics side.
