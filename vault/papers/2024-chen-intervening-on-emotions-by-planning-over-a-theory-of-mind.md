# Intervening on emotions by planning over a theory of mind

*Chen, T., Houlihan, S. D., Chandra, K., Tenenbaum, J. B., &amp; Saxe, R. (2024).* Proc. 46th CogSci, 5887–5894.

[PDF](https://chentoast.github.io/papers/emotions-cogsci-2024.pdf)

## Why it's here

Moves theory of mind from prediction to <em>intervention</em>: agents plan actions in order to change another agent's state, including joint interventions by two agents, with prediction and intervention sharing one generative model. That prediction-to-intervention step is the mechanism a joint-planning model needs, even though the second agent here is a target rather than a co-planner.
