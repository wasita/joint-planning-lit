# Taking others for granted: Balancing personal and presentational goals in action selection

*Btesh, V., Lagnado, D. A., &amp; Gerstenberg, T. (2025).* Proc. 47th CogSci.

[PDF](https://cicl.stanford.edu/papers/btesh2025taking.pdf)

## Why it's here

Agents choose actions whose value depends partly on how those actions shift an observer's beliefs about them, and observers invert that reasoning. Recursive in the same way inverse planning is, but with reputation rather than a shared goal as the object — useful as a contrast case for what joint planning adds.
