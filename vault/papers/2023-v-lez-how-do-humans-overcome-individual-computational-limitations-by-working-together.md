# How do humans overcome individual computational limitations by working together? Cognitive Science, 47, e13232

*Vélez, Christian, Hardy, Thompson & Griffiths (2023).*

[PDF](https://cocosci.princeton.edu/papers/velez2023overcome.pdf)

## Why it's here

Her best short piece for the synthesis bullet — reframes human cognition as a distributed computing system, with a particle-filtering model of populations approximating Bayesian inference via cultural accumulation. Read for framing, not for a fittable model: it's a population/multigenerational argument, not a dyadic one.
