# A social inverse-planning model captures how relationship intimacy constrains action planning and interpretation

*Chen, A. M., Thomas, A. J., Tenenbaum, J. B., &amp; Saxe, R. (2026).* Proc. 48th CogSci.

[PDF](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/90w9j48b)

## Why it's here

Inverse planning where the relationship between agents is itself a parameter shaping which actions are chosen and how they are read. Relevant to joint planning as a reminder that the utilities two agents coordinate over are not fixed by the task alone — who they are to each other changes what counts as a good joint outcome.
