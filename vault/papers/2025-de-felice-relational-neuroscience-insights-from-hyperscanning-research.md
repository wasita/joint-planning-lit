## Why it's here

The current review of dual-brain work, and unusual in engaging the mechanism-versus-epiphenomenon question about inter-brain synchrony rather than cataloguing coherence findings. Reads mutual prediction and behavioural alignment through predictive-processing accounts, which is what makes it usable next to a computational model rather than parallel to one.
