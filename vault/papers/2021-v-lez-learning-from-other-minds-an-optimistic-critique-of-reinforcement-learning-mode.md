# Learning from other minds: an optimistic critique of reinforcement learning models of social learning

*Vélez & Gweon (2021).* Current Opinion in Behavioral Sciences, 38, 110–115.

[PDF](https://sll.stanford.edu/docs/2021_Velez_Gweon_Current_Opinions.pdf)

## Why it's here

Six pages, high value-per-minute. Argues RL models of social learning oversimplify by treating social information as a lean reward-predictive cue when humans instead infer latent goals, beliefs and strategies; advocates hybrid Bayesian-ToM + RL. That is the argumentative bridge between the economics/RL framing and the cogsci-ToM framing this list sets out to synthesize.
