# Communicative eye contact signals a commitment to cooperate for young children

*Siposova, B., Tomasello, M., &amp; Carpenter, M. (2018).* Cognition, 179, 192–201.

[PDF](https://www.eva.mpg.de/documents/Elsevier/Siposova_Communicative_Cognition_2018_2608916.pdf)

## Why it's here

A single ostensive cue — mutual gaze — is enough to make young children behave as though a commitment is in force. A useful reminder that the cues carrying commitment can be far thinner than any explicit agreement.
