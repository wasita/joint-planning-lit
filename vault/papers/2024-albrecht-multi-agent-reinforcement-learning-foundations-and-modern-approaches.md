# Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning: Foundations and Modern Approaches

*Albrecht, Christianos & Schäfer (2024).* MIT Press. — Ch. 3 and 4 only.

[free book](https://www.marl-book.com/)

## Why it's here

Free, CC-licensed, solves two problems at once. Ch. 3 walks the exact ladder — normal-form → repeated → stochastic (Markov) games → POSGs. Ch. 4 is a compact game-theory bootstrap (best response, Nash, correlated equilibrium, Pareto optimality) without a semester of micro. Read it as a notation dictionary, not a textbook. Skip Ch. 5+; nothing in the joint-planning program here needs deep RL.
