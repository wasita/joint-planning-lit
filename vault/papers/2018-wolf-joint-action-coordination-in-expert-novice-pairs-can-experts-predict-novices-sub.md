# Joint action coordination in expert–novice pairs: Can experts predict novices' suboptimal timing? Cognition, 178, 103–108

*Wolf, T., Sebanz, N., &amp; Knoblich, G. (2018).*

## Why it's here

A sharp test of whether co-actors model each other's <em>planning</em> or merely entrain to each other's rhythm: experts have to predict a novice partner's suboptimal timing, which entrainment alone cannot deliver. Useful wherever a model needs to distinguish partner-modelling from coupling.
