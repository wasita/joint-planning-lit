# On commitments and other uncertainty reduction tools in joint action

*Michael, J., &amp; Pacherie, E. (2015).* Journal of Social Ontology, 1(1), 89–120.

[open access](https://doi.org/10.1515/jso-2014-0021)

## Why it's here

The framing paper for the commitment literature: commitments are treated as one of a family of devices that reduce uncertainty about what a partner will do, which makes them comparable to signalling, convention, and habit rather than sui generis. Useful for anyone trying to state precisely what a commitment adds over a shared plan.
