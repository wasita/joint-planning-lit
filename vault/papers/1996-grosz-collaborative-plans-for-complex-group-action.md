# Collaborative plans for complex group action

*Grosz, B. J., &amp; Kraus, S. (1996).* Artificial Intelligence, 86(2), 269–357.

[PDF](https://jmvidal.cse.sc.edu/library/grosz96a.pdf)

## Why it's here

The canonical AI formalization of shared plans, and a genuine alternative to joint-intention accounts. SharedPlans builds collaboration entirely out of <em>individual</em> attitudes — crucially <code>Int.Th</code>, intending <em>that</em> a proposition hold, which does not itself drive action but licenses meshing subplans, helping a partner, and keeping them informed. The authors are explicit that no unreduced notion of we-intention is required. The other half of the contribution is partiality: agents begin with incomplete recipes and no settled allocation of who does what, and elaborate the plan as they go. Long, and worth reading selectively, but the reductive-versus-primitive question it poses is still live in every model in threads B and D.
