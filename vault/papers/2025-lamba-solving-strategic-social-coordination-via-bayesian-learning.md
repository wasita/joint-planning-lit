# Solving strategic social coordination via Bayesian learning

*Lamba, A., Houlihan, S. D., &amp; Saxe, R. (2025).* Proc. 47th CogSci, 6117–6123.

[PDF](https://daeh.info/pubs/lamba2025ase.pdf)

## Why it's here

An Asymmetric Social Exchange Game with N = 675, pitting a model that learns coordination from experience against one that infers partners' hidden motivations. A clean instance of the question that runs through this whole list: how much of coordination is reinforcement over outcomes, and how much is inference about what the other agent wants.
