# Ad-hoc human-AI coordination challenge

*Dizdarević, T., Hammond, R., Gessler, T., Calinescu, A., Cook, J., Gallici, M., Lupu, A., &amp; Foerster, J. N. (2025).* ICML 2025, PMLR 267, 13900–13937.

[PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/dizdarevic25a.html)

## Why it's here

Distils a corpus of 3,079 human Hanabi games into standardized human-proxy partners behind a controlled evaluation server, so human-facing coordination can be measured reproducibly without re-running expensive human studies per algorithm. Read for the methodological argument as much as the benchmark.
