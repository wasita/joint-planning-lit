# Computational joint action: Dynamical models to understand the development of joint coordination

*De Vicariis, C., Chackochan, V. T., Bandini, L., Ravaschio, A., &amp; Sanguineti, V. (2024).* PLOS Computational Biology, 20(10), e1011948.

[open access](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011948)

## Why it's here

Human dyads, physically coupled, in a continuous two-via-point reaching task, modelled as a differential game — and tested for whether they converge on the predicted game-theoretic solutions as a function of what each partner can sense of the other. One of very few papers where continuous-time coordination with real humans is given an explicit game-theoretic treatment.
