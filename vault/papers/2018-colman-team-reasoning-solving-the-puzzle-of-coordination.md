# Team reasoning: solving the puzzle of coordination

*Colman & Gold (2018).* Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 25(5), 1770–1783.

## Why it's here

The entry point to team reasoning — written for psychologists, no game theory assumed. The economics-side cousin of the “joint intention” branch. Read it as: the same idea, thirty years earlier, in different notation. Noticing exactly these convergences is much of the payoff of reading across these traditions. (Skip Bacharach's book; read Gold & Sugden's intro to it for more.)
