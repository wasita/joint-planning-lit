# Distinct neural dynamics in joint versus side-by-side actions: Insights from dual EEG

*Fanghella, M., Mussini, E., Zazio, A., Genovese, F., Satta, E., Barchiesi, G., Battaglia-Mayer, A., Bortoletto, M., &amp; Sinigaglia, C. (2025).* Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience, 20(1), nsaf075.

[PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12401490/)

## Why it's here

Acting on a shared goal shows elevated early preparatory activity and reduced late activity relative to acting alongside someone, and the reversal tracks how predictable the partner is rather than one's own performance. A neural cost-benefit signature of committing to a collective goal.
