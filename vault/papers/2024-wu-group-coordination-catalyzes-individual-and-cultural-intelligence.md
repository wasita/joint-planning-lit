# Group coordination catalyzes individual and cultural intelligence

*Wu, Dale & Hawkins (2024).* Open Mind, 8, 1037–1057.

[PDF](https://charleywu.github.io/downloads/wu2024group.pdf)

## Why it's here

Wu's actual collective-intelligence position piece — and note the Hawkins co-authorship again. Argues the ad hoc interacting group is a distinct explanatory level between individual cognition and cumulative culture. This is the cleanest available statement of why dyad and small-group coordination is not just small-scale collective behavior, which is exactly the framing distinction this literature needs.
