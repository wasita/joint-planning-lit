# Herd those sheep: emergent multiagent coordination and behavioral-mode switching

*Nalepka, Kallen, Chemero, Saltzman & Richardson (2017).* Psychological Science, 28(5), 630–650.

## Why it's here

Continuous spatial shepherding. All pairs start with divided-labor “search-and-recover”; a subset spontaneously discover a synchronized oscillatory mode. An entirely different theoretical vocabulary — dynamical systems, attractors, relative phase, HKB — describing the same phenomenon the Bayesian models describe. Read it precisely because it doesn't share their assumptions.
