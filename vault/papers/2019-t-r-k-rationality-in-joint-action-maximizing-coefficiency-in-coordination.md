# Rationality in joint action: Maximizing coefficiency in coordination

*Török, G., Pomiechowska, B., Csibra, G., &amp; Sebanz, N. (2019).* Psychological Science, 30(6), 930–941.

[PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6560520/)

## Why it's here

&ldquo;Coefficiency&rdquo; — co-actors minimize the <em>aggregate dyadic</em> cost of an action sequence even at personal cost. The cleanest empirical rationality principle for joint plans, and the natural behavioural anchor for any resource-rational treatment of multi-agent planning. See also Török et al. (2021, <i>Open Mind</i>) on computing joint action costs.
