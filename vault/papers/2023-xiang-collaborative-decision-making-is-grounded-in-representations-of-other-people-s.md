# Collaborative decision making is grounded in representations of other people's competence and effort

*Xiang, Vélez & Gershman (2023).* JEP: General, 152, 1565–1579.

[PDF](https://gershmanlab.com/pubs/Xiang23.pdf)

## Why it's here

Vélez's most dyadic work, and the closest she gets to joint planning. In a stag hunt, “will my partner commit?” decomposes into exactly competence (can they?) and effort (will they bother?). The inference structure ports over. Caveat: turn-based offline-judgment tasks, not real-time — relevant model structure, irrelevant paradigm.
