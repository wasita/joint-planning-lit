# A Bayesian theory of mind approach to modeling cooperation and communication

*Stacy, S., Gong, S., Parab, A., Zhao, M., Jiang, K., &amp; Gao, T. (2023).* WIREs Computational Statistics, 16(1), e1631.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/wics.1631)

## Why it's here

The review-length statement of the Imagined We programme, and the best single place to see cooperation and communication derived from one framework rather than treated as separate problems. Signals are modelled as a species of rational action — chosen because of what they let a partner infer — and shared agency, rather than recursive individual reasoning, is what makes the inference tractable. Read this before the individual Imagined We papers; it supplies the scaffolding they assume.
