[PDF](https://charleywu.github.io/downloads/witt2024humans.pdf)

## Why it's here

The formal-framework paper for the synthesis bullet. Four competing model families — asocial GP-UCB, Decision Biasing, Value Shaping, Social Generalization — arbitrated by exceedance probability. SG wins. The DB/VS/SG trichotomy maps directly onto the policy-level vs. value-level vs. belief-level places a partner can be inserted into a joint-planning model. A genuinely useful carve-up of the model space.
